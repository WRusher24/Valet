# Valet — User Guide

Valet removes the morning "what do I wear?" decision by showing colour palettes that work together, with real outfit references for each one.

## Getting started

1. Open the app and choose **Create an account**. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. You get instant access — there is no approval step.
2. Use the bottom bar to move around: **Explore**, **By Item**, **Shuffle**, **Log** and **Add**.

Everything you create (custom combos, uploaded photos, saved outfits and wear history) is private to your account. The starter library is shared and read-only.

## Browse colour combinations (Explore)

- Each card shows 2–4 capsule swatches with the colour names and hex codes, a season tag and an outfit count.
- Use the filter pills at the top: **All**, **Summer / Warm Weather** or **Winter / Cold Weather**. All-Season palettes appear under both.
- Tap a card to open its gallery: full-outfit photos with a garment breakdown (jacket, top, bottom, shoes), a season tag and action buttons.
- **Save** keeps an outfit in your Saved tab. **Wore This Today** opens a date field (today by default — change it to log an earlier day) and records it in your history.

## Find outfits by one piece (By Item)

1. Pick a garment: **Top / Shirt**, **Jacket / Blazer / Coat**, **Pants / Trousers** or **Shoes**.
2. Pick a broad colour family: Browns, Blues, Greys, Greens, Whites & Creams, Blacks, Burgundy & Reds, or Pastels & Other. Each family covers many shades (Browns includes camel, tan, cognac, chocolate, espresso, taupe and khaki).
3. You instantly see every matching palette and outfit. The matching garment is highlighted in each breakdown.

Example: **Pants / Trousers + Browns** shows every outfit with any shade of brown, tan or camel on the bottom half.

## Shuffle

Tap **Shuffle Combo** on Explore, or open the **Shuffle** tab, optionally choose a weather filter, and Valet opens a random palette's full gallery. Shuffle again for another idea.

## Create your own combo (Add → Custom Combo)

1. Choose 2, 3 or 4 colours.
2. For each colour use the colour picker, type a name and confirm its colour family (Valet suggests one automatically).
3. Pick a season: Summer, Winter or All-Season, then **Save Combination**.

Your combo appears at the top of Explore with a "Yours" tag. Open it to delete it later if you wish.

## Upload your own outfit photos (Add → Outfit Photo)

1. Choose any combination — starter or your own — and a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo (large photos are shrunk automatically).
2. Add a short title and pick Summer or Winter.
3. Tag the pieces visible in the photo: choose a colour family for each of Top, Jacket/Layer, Pants and Shoes and add a short description such as "Sand Chinos". Leave a piece as **Not visible** to skip it.
4. Tap **Upload Outfit**.

Tagged pieces automatically feed the **By Item** filter. Your photos are visible only to you, and you can delete them from the outfit card.

## Log: wear history and saved outfits

- **Worn History** lists every outfit you logged, newest first, with the date and palette. Remove a mistaken entry with **Remove**.
- **Saved** shows all outfits you favourited.
- Outfit cards and combo pages show when you last wore that outfit or palette, so you can rotate your wardrobe.

## My Closet: your real wardrobe

The **Closet** tab turns Valet from inspiration into a tool for what you actually own. Everything in it is private to your account.

### Add your pieces
1. Open **Closet → + Add Item**.
2. Tap **Take photo** (opens the camera on a phone) or **Choose from library**.
3. Pick a category: Top, Bottom, Outerwear, Shoes, Belt, Watch or Bracelet.
4. Type a name (for example "Brown Suede Loafers" or "Chronograph Watch") and choose a broad colour family.
5. Tap **Add to Closet**. Open any item later to edit its details, swap its photo or delete it.

Use the category pills at the top of the closet (All, Tops, Bottoms, Outerwear, Shoes, Belts, Watches, Bracelets) to filter your inventory.

### Build an outfit from your own pieces
**Closet → Build Outfit** shows your photos for every slot: one top, bottom, pair of shoes, belt, watch and bracelet, plus optional outerwear. Tap a photo to select it, or tap **Smart-fill from my closet** to start from a colour-matched suggestion. The palette at the top updates as you choose. Saved outfits appear under **Closet → My Outfits**, with the exact photos of your pieces next to the palette.

### Smart shuffle from your closet
Open **Shuffle → Shuffle from My Closet**, choose a weather filter and tap the button. Valet builds a full outfit from your real items, matching colours (for example, the belt always follows the shoes when it can). Tap **Shuffle again** for another idea or **Save this outfit** to keep it. If a slot is empty, Valet tells you which category to add.

### Match one piece
**Closet → Find Matches** (or "Or pick a piece from my closet" on the By Item tab) lets you pick one specific belt, watch, shirt or pair of shoes. You instantly see compatible pieces in your closet, your saved outfits that include it, and lookbook palettes and outfits that suit it.

### Deleting
Deleting an item also deletes any saved outfit that used it, because an outfit can't have an empty slot. Valet warns you with the number of affected outfits first.
